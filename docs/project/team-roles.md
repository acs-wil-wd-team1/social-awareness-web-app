## Repository ownership

The Charter confirms roles but does not assign individual features, documents or repository folders.

Until the team confirms the Stage 2 allocation, folders show where each type of work belongs rather than who has accepted it.

## Contribution record

Each team member should upload or commit their own contribution using their own account.

GitHub history supports contribution evidence, but the official allocation should still be recorded in the meeting minutes and Jira.
